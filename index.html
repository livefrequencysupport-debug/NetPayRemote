Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know as a US remote contractor

What is Form W-8BEN and why do I need it?

W-8BEN is a US IRS form that non-US contractors fill out to claim tax treaty benefits (DTAA). Without it, US clients legally must withhold 30% of your payment as federal tax. With a signed W-8BEN, this drops to 0% for most treaty countries like India, Philippines, UK, Pakistan. You keep 30% more income instantly.

Is Wise really better than Payoneer or bank wire?

Yes, in almost all scenarios. Wise uses real mid-market exchange rates with a tiny transparent fee (0.5%–0.8%). Payoneer charges a hidden 2%–3% markup on FX rates plus transfer fees. Traditional SWIFT bank wires cost $25–$50 per transfer plus poor intermediary bank conversion rates.

How does Section 44ADA work in India for freelancers?

Under Section 44ADA of the Indian Income Tax Act, eligible professionals (software developers, designers, consultants) earning up to ₹75 Lakhs/year can declare 50% of gross income as flat tax-free expenses. You only pay tax on the remaining 50%, dramatically reducing your effective local tax rate to ~6%-12%.

What is Skydo and why is it recommended for Indian contractors?

Skydo provides local US bank account details for non-US contractors. They charge a flat $19 per transaction with zero FX markup, making it vastly cheaper than PayPal or traditional banks for payouts over $1,500/month. Additionally, they issue automatic digital FIRA certificates required for Indian IT compliance.

How often are exchange rates updated?

NetPayRemote fetches live exchange rates directly every 30 minutes via public FX API endpoints. Rates are cached locally to ensure sub-second rendering speeds without rate-limiting interruptions.

Do I need to pay US federal taxes if I live outside the USA?

If you are not a US citizen/green card holder and perform all your work physically outside the US, your earnings are classified as non-US source income. Provided you submit a valid W-8BEN form, you owe 0% US federal income tax. You only pay tax to your home country's tax authority.
